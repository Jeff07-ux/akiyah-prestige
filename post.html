A first evening in the medina.

Guides6 min read12 Sep 2026

In this article

Begin above the cityWalk with a purposeEnd the evening somewhere quiet

Most visitors meet the medina at its busiest hour, in the heat of the afternoon, with a phone in one hand and a map that no longer makes sense. We suggest the opposite: arrive as the light softens, an hour before the call to prayer, with someone who knows which door to push.

Begin above the city

The best introduction to the old city is from a rooftop. From above, the maze becomes a pattern: minarets, palm tops, the Atlas on a clear day. Our guests usually start on a private terrace with mint tea, while their guide explains the neighbourhoods they will walk through.

The medina rewards patience. Walk slowly, and the city starts to open its doors.

Walk with a purpose

Rather than covering everything, choose one quarter and one craft. A morning with a leather worker, an evening with a lantern maker. The souk changes completely when an artisan invites you into the workshop behind the stall.

End the evening somewhere quiet

Dinner is best in a courtyard, away from the square. We reserve tables in a handful of riads where the cooking is careful and the guests are few. Your chauffeur waits at the nearest gate, and the walk back is lit by our team.

Arrange this evening

Rooftop tea, a historian guide, dinner in a courtyard.
