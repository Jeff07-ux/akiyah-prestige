## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, weighted equally:

- **Travellers:** discerning international guests (couples, families, small private groups) planning a private stay in Marrakech and across Morocco. They want stays, arrivals, experiences and a single point of contact, arranged without effort on their side.
- **Event clients:** people planning a wedding or private celebration in Marrakech, served under the Aliyah Events name, from first idea to the last guest home.

Both arrive to decide whether to trust Aliyah Prestige with something personal and expensive, then start a conversation, usually on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Aliyah Prestige is a private concierge service in Marrakech. It arranges bespoke stays, journeys, experiences and events with absolute discretion. The website exists to earn that trust and turn it into an enquiry. Success is a guest or event client sending a first message (WhatsApp first, email second) and receiving two or three options within the hour.

## Positioning

Three things together, which a neighbouring concierge cannot truthfully claim at once:

1. **Family and local access.** A Marrakech family business founded in 1986. Opens doors others cannot: artisans' workshops before the medina wakes, private gardens, family kitchens, homes and riads.
2. **Discretion first.** Names, plans and guests stay private. Only vetted drivers, guides and venues. Trusted by public figures.
3. **End-to-end scope with one contact.** One concierge covers everything from private jets and helicopters to stays, experiences, gastronomy, wellness and weddings. A real person answers at any hour, in the guest's language.

## Operating Context

- Enquiry starts on WhatsApp ("where from, when, how many is enough"); email is the secondary channel. Reply promise: within the hour, 24/7.
- Service categories: Arrivals & Travel (jet charter, helicopter transfers, chauffeur and fleet, VIP meet and assist), Stays (private villas, riads, palaces, desert camps), Experiences, Gastronomy, Wellness, Events & Concierge (Aliyah Events).
- Signature experiences: helicopter over the Atlas, dinner under the stars in the Agafay, exclusive souk access, family cooking class, a private hour in a secret garden, private chauffeur for the whole stay.
- Site sections: home, services, gallery, journal (articles), about.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static website generated by `node build.mjs`. Shared copy lives in `collection/content.mjs`; styles in `site.css`, `motion.css`; behaviour in `site.js`, `motion.js`. Edit sources, then rebuild; do not hand-edit generated HTML.
- Language: English only today.
- Multiple user-selectable colour themes exist on the site.
- Terminology: "concierge", "guests", "stays", "Aliyah Events" for the events arm.
- **Open:** phone number, email address and WhatsApp link are placeholders until the client confirms them. Figures, flight times, aircraft specs, dates and addresses in sample copy are also placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Aliyah Prestige; events arm: Aliyah Events. Based in Marrakech.
- Voice: calm, warm, precise, understated. Short sentences. Concrete details over adjectives. Never boastful about price or exclusivity in loud terms.
- Discretion is part of the brand: guest names stay private unless they have approved publication.

## Product Principles

1. **One person, one conversation.** Every path leads to talking with a real concierge; never to a form maze or a booking engine.
2. **Proof through specifics.** Trust comes from real, concrete detail (places, people, heritage), not superlatives.
3. **Discretion is visible.** The site itself behaves discreetly: no exposing guests, no pushy urgency.
4. **Access over inventory.** Sell what only this family can open, not a catalogue anyone could list.
5. **Stays and events carry equal weight.** Neither audience should feel like an afterthought.
