Our culinary experiences

Marrakech is a city where Moroccan gastronomy reaches its peak: a destination celebrated for its vibrant flavours, refined traditions, and exquisite variety of dishes. From delicate appetisers and savoury tagines to sweet pastries and gourmet desserts, the city offers a sensory experience like no other.

At Aliyah Prestige, we transform this renowned culinary heritage into a VIP service designed exclusively for our distinguished guests. Whether you wish to savour an intimate dinner for two, a family gathering, or a grand event, we guarantee access to the finest restaurants in Marrakech, from celebrated venues to the city’s most exclusive hidden gems.

We handpick the finest restaurants, chefs, and artisans, curating bespoke experiences: a candlelit dinner in the desert, a refined banquet with Andalusian musicians, or a private chef in your residence.

Our culinary experiences

  • Exclusive restaurant reservationsPriority access to the most sought-after fine dining tables in Marrakech.
  • Private chef in your residenceRenowned Moroccan or international chefs prepare a bespoke menu tailored to your tastes, right in your villa or riad.
  • Immersive cooking classesA hands-on experience where couples, families, or groups learn the secrets of Moroccan cuisine, a true blend of culture and entertainment.
  • Unique dining settingsFrom candlelit dinners in hidden riads to chic desert banquets under the stars, every meal is elevated into an unforgettable memory.

With Aliyah Prestige, gastronomy becomes more than dining: it becomes an art of living. We do not simply book a table or hire a chef; we create culinary journeys crafted around your desires, delivered with elegance, discretion, and unmatched attention to detail.

Choosing Aliyah Prestige means more than choosing a concierge. It means entrusting your palate and your precious moments to a concierge that values authenticity, exclusivity, and luxury service of the highest standard.
