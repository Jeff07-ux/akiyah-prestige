The Moroccan rituals of excellenceExclusive locationsThe Aliyah Prestige promise

In Marrakech, wellness is not just a treatment: it is a sacred ritual, a timeless art perfected through centuries. At Aliyah Prestige, we open the doors to the most prestigious and hidden sanctuaries of the Red City, where wellness becomes a journey of rebirth.

From the moment you enter, the world outside disappears. The air is perfumed with orange blossom and rose water, the soft glow of lanterns reflects on intricate zellige tiles, and you are enveloped in an atmosphere worthy of One Thousand and One Nights.

The Moroccan rituals of excellence

  • Traditional hammam ceremonyBegin with the quintessential Moroccan ritual, a purifying steam bath followed by exfoliation with savon noir, ghassoul clay masks, and rose-infused rinses.
  • Royal massage experienceDrift into serenity with massages performed by expert practitioners, from deep tissue to aromatic oil blends, crafted for ultimate relaxation.
  • Signature Moroccan treatmentsIndulge in saffron facials, argan oil body wraps, and desert-sand scrubs, each harnessing Morocco’s natural treasures.
  • Holistic spa journeysExperience immersive rituals combining sound therapy, herbal infusions, and essential oils that harmonise body and spirit.

Every detail is curated to create not just a spa visit, but an unforgettable immersion into Moroccan wellness culture, elevated with exclusivity and discretion.

Exclusive locations

We give you privileged access to Marrakech’s most select and prestigious spas: serene riads hidden behind ornate doors, luxury resorts surrounded by lush palm gardens, and private wellness sanctuaries reserved for an elite few. Each space is chosen for its authenticity, refinement, and ability to make you forget the outside world.

The Aliyah Prestige promise

With us, wellness is transformed into an art of rebirth. Our clients leave as if newly born: their senses awakened, their body renewed, and their mind liberated.

This is not a simple spa service. It is a magical escape, curated for the world’s most discerning travellers, delivered with Moroccan elegance and world-class excellence.
