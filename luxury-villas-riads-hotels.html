What we offerWhy choose Aliyah Prestige for your stay

From majestic palace hotels to hidden riads tucked away in the Medina, Aliyah Prestige secures the most exclusive accommodations in Marrakech, handpicked to match your preferences, expectations and desires.

We eliminate the hassle of comparison and booking. Instead, we curate your perfect stay from A to Z: the ideal location, the finest suite, seamless check-in, and personalised in-room details. Every element is orchestrated for you.

What we offer

  • Bespoke accommodation matchingWhether you’re seeking a luxury villa in the Palmeraie, a romantic riad in the Medina, or a 5-star hotel in Hivernage, we select properties that reflect your taste and standards.
  • Direct partnerships with top-tier establishmentsIncluding Royal Mansour, La Mamounia, Amanjena, Mandarin Oriental, Selman Marrakech, El Fenn and Villa des Orangers.
  • Preferred guest status and upgradesThanks to our trusted relationships with general managers and hotel directors.
  • Hosted by dedicated staffPersonal chefs, butlers, housekeepers, security, concierge 24/7.
  • All-inclusive or à la carte packagesTailored to your lifestyle.
  • Special occasions handled with graceHoneymoon, anniversary, birthday, proposal.
  • Exclusive touchesFloral arrangements, personalised gifts, welcome amenities, champagne on arrival.
  • In-room services on requestSpa treatments, personal chef, butler, security personnel.

We liaise directly with the hotel or villa team to ensure that every special request is fulfilled before your arrival: discreetly, efficiently, and with taste.
