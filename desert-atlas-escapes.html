Just beyond the vibrant energy of Marrakech lies a world straight out of a fairytale: majestic deserts, snow-capped peaks, hidden valleys, and starlit skies. At Aliyah Prestige, we curate extraordinary escapes into the Agafay Desert, the golden dunes of Merzouga, and the breathtaking Atlas Mountains, each designed to immerse you in a realm of wonder, luxury, and authenticity.

The Agafay Desert: a golden getaway near Marrakech

Only a short drive from Marrakech, the Agafay Desert offers a serene yet adventurous escape. Imagine:

  • Riding a camel as the sun melts into the horizon
  • The thrill of quad biking or buggy rides over rolling hills
  • A private dinner under the stars, complete with traditional Moroccan folklore, fire shows, and live music

Merzouga and Sahara luxury bivouacs

For those who dream bigger, fly across the horizon to the legendary Sahara dunes of Merzouga, a journey reserved for the few, where VIP service transforms the desert into your private kingdom.

  • Stay in luxurious private bivouacs, styled like palaces of One Thousand and One Nights, where every detail is curated for your comfort.
  • Marvel at postcard-perfect sand dunes, monumental sunsets, and a starlit sky like nowhere else on Earth.
  • Arrive in styleby luxury SUV, or in minutes by private helicopter, accompanied by our professional staff for a seamless VIP journey.

Here, you will feel as if the world belongs only to you.

The Atlas Mountains: a journey through nature and culture

Venture into the Atlas Mountains, where winding valleys, cascading waterfalls, and authentic Berber villages reveal the soul of Morocco.

  • Hike or ride through scenic trails with panoramic views
  • Meet locals and discover the traditions of mountain life
  • Enjoy a gourmet picnic by a waterfall or a private lunch in a secluded kasbah

This is not just an excursion: it is an immersion into Morocco’s natural beauty and cultural depth.

Why choose Aliyah Prestige

Because we don’t just organise excursions: we create masterpieces of travel. Every journey is private, bespoke, and secured with luxury transport or helicopter options. We collaborate only with the finest partners, exclusive camps, and hidden gems. With us, every moment feels effortless, elegant, and unforgettable.

Discover what no guidebook can offer: a rare combination of authentic adventure and refined luxury, curated for the world’s most discerning travellers.
