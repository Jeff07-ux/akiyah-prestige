Marrakech is one of the most sought-after wedding destinations in the world, and we ensure yours becomes unforgettable. Your wedding becomes a refined production: seamless, emotional, unforgettable.

  • We handleFull wedding planning and timeline, venue sourcing (exclusive and private locations), creative direction and scenography, floral design and installations, luxury catering and menu curation, entertainment and live performances, guest logistics and hospitality, and on-site coordination from start to finish.

Private celebrations and exclusive events

We design ultra-personalised celebrations for discerning clients who expect more than “just a party”: milestone birthdays, luxury anniversary dinners, marriage proposals, VIP private dinners, baby showers, engagement parties, high-end themed events, and celebrity and influencer private gatherings.

Every detail is curated: lighting, atmosphere, music, table styling, signature moments, surprise elements. You enjoy the celebration. We control the precision behind the scenes.

Corporate events and executive experiences

Aliyah Prestige is also a strategic partner for companies seeking a corporate event planner in Morocco. We organise executive seminars in Marrakech, corporate retreats and board meetings, leadership off-sites, product launches, brand activations, press and media events, gala dinners, awards ceremonies, incentive travel programmes and end-of-year celebrations.

Our difference: we combine event production with luxury concierge services: VIP airport handling, private transfers, premium accommodation, curated experiences for guests. One partner. One standard of excellence.

Our signature approach

  • Full-service planning, end to endWe manage your project from concept to execution: timeline, suppliers, production, guest logistics, on-site coordination and event-day direction.

Through our trusted network and long-standing relationships, we organise high-end events in iconic venues such as Royal Mansour Marrakech, La Mamounia, Amanjena, Mandarin Oriental Marrakech, Selman Marrakech and Fairmont Royal Palm Marrakech, each chosen to match your vision, guest profile, aesthetic direction and logistical requirements.
  • Full budget management and transparencyLuxury must feel effortless. We structure your event budget strategically and manage it with rigour: clear allocation, supplier negotiation, ongoing tracking, no hidden surprises. You maintain clarity and control while we optimise every detail.
