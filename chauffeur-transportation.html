Every element, designed with finesse

From the moment your plane touches the tarmac, you are no longer a traveller. You are a guest of honour, personally welcomed airside by our VIP escort service: the very beginning of your VIP Marrakech experience.

Your journey begins with a seamless airport experience, private immigration assistance, and direct access to your chauffeured vehicle, a luxury car chosen to reflect your standards. No queues, no waiting.

Throughout your stay, your private driver and certified local expert, your dedicated Marrakech guide, remain at your service 24/7. Discreet, multilingual, and trained to the highest standards, they anticipate every detail before you speak it.

Every element, designed with finesse

  • Exclusive access to monuments and museums, far from the crowds
  • Authentic, bespoke Moroccan experiences with certified cultural experts
  • Private museum visits and behind-the-scenes moments in heritage sites
  • Fine dining reservations at Marrakech’s most coveted tables
  • Last-minute itinerary adjustments handled with elegance

More than a private tour of Marrakech, your experience expands beyond the Red City: private dinners under the stars in the Agafay desert, visits to hidden riads, helicopter journeys over the Atlas.

Should you need enhanced privacy or safety, a discreet professional bodyguard can accompany you, an optional addition to your concierge experience. Every detail is handled with artistry, from floral scents in your car to the table you’re offered at lunch.
