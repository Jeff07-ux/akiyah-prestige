  • A global private jet network

    Access to thousands of vetted aircraft and operators worldwide, connected through our aviation partner’s international platform and fleet presence in Morocco.

  • Morocco-based expertise

    Aircraft based at Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport and regular operations to Marrakech, Rabat, Agadir, Tangier, Fès and Ouarzazate mean optimised routing and faster solutions for flights to and within Morocco.

  • Discreet, UHNW-oriented service

    We are used to HNW and UHNW travellers, executives and celebrities: discretion, security and confidentiality are non-negotiable.

  • 24/7 availability

    Urgent same-day departure or last-minute schedule change? A combined aviation and concierge team is reachable around the clock.

  • One point of contact

    You don’t need to speak to operators, FBOs or handling agents. Aliyah Prestige coordinates everything and reports only what you need to know.

How the experience feels

  • 01

    You contact Aliyah Prestige

    Share your city of departure, preferred dates, number of passengers and any specific needs (pets, smoking / non-smoking, children, security, special occasion).

  • 02

    You receive a curated proposal

    One clear proposal, or a short list of 2–3 aircraft options, each with estimated flight time and schedule, aircraft category and photos, indicative pricing and what is included, and suggested ground transfers and add-ons (villa, hotel, desert camp, restaurants).

  • 03

    You confirm, we activate the aviation team

    Once you approve, we coordinate directly with our aviation partner and operators to secure the aircraft, crew, slots and permits.

  • 04

    Seamless arrival in Marrakech or anywhere in Morocco

    Upon landing, you are greeted, escorted through VIP / FBO facilities, and your driver is waiting planeside or at the dedicated terminal. From that moment on, you’re in the Aliyah Prestige universe: villa or palace check-in, private chef, spa, restaurants, nightlife, desert experiences, personal shopping and more.
