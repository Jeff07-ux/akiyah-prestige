More than a jet: a fully curated Morocco experience

Most “private jet” websites stop at the flight. Aliyah Prestige connects your jet, your ground transfers, your villa, your restaurants and your experiences in Marrakech into one seamless, luxury journey. With Aliyah Prestige, you benefit from:

  • A global private jet networkAccess to thousands of vetted aircraft and operators worldwide, connected through our aviation partner’s international platform and fleet presence in Morocco.
  • Morocco-based expertiseAircraft based at Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport and regular operations to Marrakech, Rabat, Agadir, Tangier, Fès and Ouarzazate mean optimised routing and faster solutions for flights to and within Morocco.
  • Discreet, UHNW-oriented serviceWe are used to HNW and UHNW travellers, executives and celebrities: discretion, security and confidentiality are non-negotiable.
  • 24/7 availabilityUrgent same-day departure or last-minute schedule change? A combined aviation and concierge team is reachable around the clock.
  • One point of contactYou don’t need to speak to operators, FBOs or handling agents. Aliyah Prestige coordinates everything and reports only what you need to know.

Private jet to Marrakech and Morocco

We organise private jet flights:

  • To MoroccoFrom Europe, UK, Middle East / GCC, USA, Africa and beyond.
  • Within MoroccoMarrakech, Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier, Agadir, Fès, Ouarzazate and selected remote airfields.

Typical routes include (one-way or return):

  • Paris – Marrakech
  • London – Marrakech
  • Geneva – Marrakech
  • Dubai / Riyadh / Doha – Marrakech
  • New York – Casablanca or Marrakech (with long-range jets)
  • Domestic hops (e.g. Marrakech – Casablanca – Laayoune – Agadir)

If your destination is not listed, our team will study your route and propose the most efficient solution.

Types of jets available

Through our aviation partner and their extended operator network, we can offer the full spectrum of aircraft, from quick weekend escapes to long-haul intercontinental flights:

  • Very Light and Light JetsIdeal for 2–6 passengers on short to mid-range routes (e.g. Madrid – Marrakech, Casablanca – Marrakech, Lisbon – Marrakech).
  • Midsize and Super Midsize JetsPerfect for business trips and family holidays across Europe, the Middle East and North Africa.
  • Heavy and Ultra Long Range JetsFor transatlantic and long-range flights (e.g. New York – Casablanca, Doha – Marrakech) in full luxury with lie-flat seating and generous cabin space.
  • VIP Airliners (on request)For large groups, weddings, incentives or corporate events travelling together to Morocco.

On each request, we shortlist a selection of aircraft with the year of manufacture and refurbishment details, seating configuration and luggage capacity, range and flight time estimates, photos and cabin layout. You choose the option that best matches your comfort, budget and image; we handle everything else.

Safety, compliance and reliability

Your safety is our starting point, not a marketing line. All flights are operated by certified air carriers complying with national and international aviation regulations (AOC / Part 135 / EASA equivalents depending on the jurisdiction). Aircraft and crew are sourced through our trusted aviation partner’s global network, known for:

  • Rigorous operator selection and ongoing performance monitoring
  • Experienced flight crews used to VIP missions
  • Strict maintenance and safety oversight
  • Full operational coordination (slots, permits, handling, fuel, ground support)

Aliyah Prestige adds an extra layer of client-side protection: we defend your interests on pricing, routing and conditions, and we only present options that we would confidently book for ourselves or our closest family.

How the experience feels

  • 1. You contact Aliyah PrestigeShare your city of departure, preferred dates, number of passengers and any specific needs (pets, smoking / non-smoking, children, security, special occasion).
  • 2. You receive a curated proposalOne clear proposal, or a short list of 2–3 aircraft options, each with estimated flight time and schedule, aircraft category and photos, indicative pricing and what is included, and suggested ground transfers and add-ons (villa, hotel, desert camp, restaurants).
  • 3. You confirm, we activate the aviation teamOnce you approve, we coordinate directly with our aviation partner and operators to secure the aircraft, crew, slots and permits.
  • 4. Seamless arrival in Marrakech or anywhere in MoroccoUpon landing, you are greeted, escorted through VIP / FBO facilities, and your driver is waiting planeside or at the dedicated terminal. From that moment on, you’re in the Aliyah Prestige universe: villa or palace check-in, private chef, spa, restaurants, nightlife, desert experiences, personal shopping and more.

Additional capabilities

On request, through our aviation partner, we can also support more complex missions:

  • Last-minute relocations and urgent business travel
  • Medical repatriation / medevac coordination to or from Morocco
  • Cargo and special equipment flights
  • High-risk or sensitive routes, with reinforced security and diplomacy handling, studied on a case-by-case basis

These missions are handled with total confidentiality and often require a phone discussion rather than email.

Tell us your departure city, your dates and approximate time, the number of passengers and any special request. Your concierge will come back with a tailored private jet proposal and, if you wish, a complete itinerary for your stay in Marrakech and beyond.
